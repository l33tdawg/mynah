Mynah

Tell it once. Reach it anywhere.

Mynah runs on your Mac, remembers across conversations, and answers in your Signal Note to Self. Local by default. No Mynah account.

What changes

Stop introducing yourself to your assistant.

It remembers across conversations, not just inside one.

“Dad’s appointment moved to the 12th.”

Still true in October, without you saying it again.

Remembered

“The flight is 6:40, not 6:14.”

The correction sticks. The wrong one does not come back.

Remembered

No re-briefing. No pasted context. Read, search, or delete anything it remembers.

How you reach it

Already in the thread you use.

Message yourself

Mynah links like Signal Desktop. No new number, no bot, no new phone app.

Or just talk

Send a voice note, or send //call and have a live conversation.

What it takes off you

It keeps track, so you can stop.

Hand it something and it stays handed over — the booking to chase, the thing to renew before the 30th, the question you asked it to go and answer.

A board, not a promise

Planned, in progress, done — see what it is actually holding.

One front door for your SAGE agents

Ask Mynah to hand work to another agent and bring the result back.

What it can do

Ask for the thing. Get the thing.

Not a chat window that describes what it would do. It writes the file, keeps what you send it, looks things up, and tells you when something moves.

It writes real documents

“Make me a PDF about the quarter.” A PDF, a Word file or a deck comes back as a file — on Signal as an attachment, in the app as something to click.

Made on your Mac

Send it anything

Photos, receipts, screenshots, PDFs. It files what you send and can bring it back when you ask for it months later.

Kept on your Mac

It looks things up

Prices, opening hours, what happened this morning. The current answer rather than what a model remembers from training.

Search terms leave

It tells you when something changes

Turn it on and it checks on its own — a message from one of your agents, a task that moved, a deadline getting close — and writes to you only when something has. Not what you just changed yourself.

Off until you turn it on
