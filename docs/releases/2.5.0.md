# Mynah 2.5.0

Mynah now supports an Even G2 companion: speak through your glasses and read the answer there, using the same Mynah conversation you already have in Signal or WhatsApp Notes to Self.

## Even G2 companion (beta)

- Your glasses use the existing self-chat, SAGE context, tasks, search, and tools. Questions and answers also appear in that chat.
- Pair once from Mynah Settings → General → Your phone → Pair G2. Choose your linked Signal or WhatsApp chat and copy the private link into the companion. The companion remembers the authorization and reconnects automatically; the Mac restores the pairing after restart.
- Quiet Listening, Sending, and Working states show progress. Submitted requests keep running when you leave the companion; reopening retrieves the result without repeating the request.
- G2 pairing is separate from temporary voice-call links. Choose Unpair in Mynah Settings to revoke access without restarting the Mac app.
- The companion includes About, author, support, privacy, and terms information, plus handling for glasses and R1 input delivered through the SDK's different event types.

Install the companion through Even Hub beta testing. It requires an Even G2, Even app 2.2.9 or newer, an online Mac running this version of Mynah, and your configured Signal or WhatsApp self-chat. The companion package is distributed separately from the Mac app.

The initial voice-to-answer flow has been tested on G2. The updated gesture handling and locked-phone reconnection are still under hardware verification. The companion cannot read other apps' inboxes or reply to mirrored notifications. Answers appear inside Mynah and your self-chat; they do not overlay other glasses apps.

The bundled SAGE version remains 11.18.27.
