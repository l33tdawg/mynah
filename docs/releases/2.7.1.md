# Mynah 2.7.1

Scheduled work is now a thing you can see and switch off on the Mac — and a
thing you can just ask for instead of learning a command.

## What 2.7.0 got wrong

2.7.0 gave Mynah a clock and an owner-facing command: `//schedule every day at
8am: check my inbox and tell me what's waiting`. What it did not give him was
anywhere to look at what was set up, anywhere to switch it off, or any way to ask
for it the way he actually talks. The verdict, in his words:

> it needs to be set up in the app itself then its just available to the user in
> conversation bro

> so that way user can go in and turn off tasks or kill those poll jobs

## Scheduled, in the window

A new destination, second in the bar after Home: everything on a clock, with the
cadence and when each last ran, and two controls that mean two different things.

* **The switch holds one.** Stop for now, keep the wording, and the clock starts
  again when you switch it back on — so pausing something for a fortnight does
  not come back as a fortnight of catch-up.
* **The cross kills one.** The request is gone, and so is what it said.

There is a form that sets work up from the Mac as well, and the list is a reader
of the same file the daemon runs from — ask for something on your phone and it
walks into the list within a few seconds.

## Just ask for it

The other half: "check my inbox every morning at 8" now sets it up, in the
thread, in this window, or on a call. **The time is still read by code and never
by the model.** The tool hands your own phrase to the same reader the command
uses, so a model cannot invent a cadence: ask for "every day at 8" and it comes
back asking whether you meant am or pm, because that is what the reader says
about a bare hour.

Stopping one stays on the Mac, deliberately. Mynah will not cancel standing work
from the thread — a small model picking the right row of a numbered list it read
a turn ago is a worse interface than a switch you can see, and getting it wrong
silently stops the wrong job.

## The one tool this cost, and what paid for it

The local model's tool catalogue has been at its measured ceiling since 21
August, and adding a tool to it is supposed to cost a re-measurement rather than
an argument. So it was re-run, on the model Mynah ships as its default, with the
real prompt and the live node's schemas — twice, differing only in whether the
new tool is in the catalogue:

    composed                             21  22  23  24  25  26  28  29  33  34  40  41
    without the new tool                 10  10  10   -  10   -  10   -   8   -   9   -
    with it — what this ships             -  10  10  10   -  10   -  10   -   9   -   9

At the size this release ships — 22 tools composed — both runs read 10 of 12.
Carrying the owner's own tool costs nothing this can measure, and the flat region
simply moved one name along with the cliff.

## Validation

2,642 Mac tests executed with zero failures (18 skipped), including the four
live-node tests against the SAGE on this Mac. The Mac app is Developer ID signed,
Apple notarized and stapled, and the disk image verifies on checksum, signature,
Gatekeeper and ticket.

The bundled SAGE version remains **11.19.22**; the Even G2 companion is unchanged
at 0.2.9.
