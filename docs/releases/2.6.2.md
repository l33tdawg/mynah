# Mynah 2.6.2

Mynah can read the pictures you send it — and now it says so, where you would
actually see it.

## The sentence that was missing

Vision shipped in 2.6.0 and the only places it was mentioned were the Privacy
screen, the model picker and the release notes. The screen every owner sits in
front of still said only *"send it a voice note from your phone"*, so the most
useful thing about the product was something you had to read the documentation
to find.

The home screen's empty state now offers a photo, and says what to do with one:
*"Photograph the thing you are asking about and it will tell you what it is."*
The line under the composer says the same, more briefly.

**And it is conditional, which is the part worth stating.** Mynah reads images
on every brain it ships — the local one is multimodal and every offered provider
has a vision model on its Quick row — but an owner who has pulled a text-only
model and pointed Mynah at it gets a brain with no eyes. Telling them to send a
photo would then be promising something the appliance answers with *"saved, but
I can't look at pictures."* So the appliance publishes whether its brain can see,
the window reads that, and the photo clause only appears when it is true. The
empty state already followed this rule for readiness; it now follows it for
capability.

An older `appliance-status.json` — written before that field existed — is read
as blind rather than discarded, so the rest of what it reports (which provider,
whether the words stay on the Mac) keeps working instead of the window falling
back to "nobody has answered a phone here".
