Mynah G2 privacy notice

Effective September 5, 2026. Publisher: Dhillon Kannabhiran (l33tdawg). Contact: dhillon@levelupctf.com. Project and source code.

What the companion does

Mynah G2 connects your Even G2 glasses to Mynah running on your Mac. Recording starts when you choose to record and stops when you submit, cancel, exit, or reach the one-minute limit. Audio travels through your phone to your Mac over encrypted WebRTC. The Mac transcribes it and handles it through your existing Signal or WhatsApp Notes to Self conversation. Your question and answer appear in that chat; the answer also appears on your glasses.

Connections and services

The call.sage.delivery relay exchanges connection setup data. If a direct connection is unavailable, TURN relays encrypted media traffic. These services receive connection metadata such as IP addresses and connection timing. Your configured model provider and tools may receive text or other information needed for model inference, web search, or actions you request. SAGE supplies context, memory, and task storage under your existing configuration. Signal, WhatsApp, Even, and your chosen providers operate under their own privacy policies.

Storage and deletion

The companion keeps the connection link in Even app local storage so it can reconnect. Forget pairing clears this saved link on the phone. Send //g2 unpair in your linked Notes to Self chat to revoke the pairing on your Mac. The Mac saves the pairing token and linked self-chat address in a private file so authorization survives restarts. It remains authorized until revoked. Treat the link as private: it grants access to that Mynah connection. The companion does not persist recordings or chat history on your phone and includes no advertising or analytics SDK.

On the Mac, submitted recordings use a temporary file that Mynah attempts to delete after request completion or failure. A crash may leave temporary files behind. The latest answer and pending request state remain in process memory until replaced or the daemon restarts. Messages, model history, SAGE memory and tasks, backups, and diagnostic logs follow your existing Mynah and service configuration; they are not deleted by disconnecting the glasses. Delete retained chat, memory, tasks, and logs using the relevant service or your Mac. Infrastructure logs may retain connection metadata according to server configuration.

Permissions and scope

Microphone access captures your explicit recording. Network access connects to your Mynah bridge. The companion does not read other apps' inboxes or mirrored notifications and does not provide replies to third-party contacts.
