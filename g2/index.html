Permissions & help
      
Check the glasses microphone connection or get help with local weather access.

      
Glasses microphone: Mynah records from your glasses through Even. A failed start can mean the glasses connection is unavailable; it does not by itself mean permission was denied. Reopen Mynah in Even and retry.

      
Location: on iPhone, open Settings → Apps → Even → Location and allow access while using the app. On Android, open Settings → Apps → Even → Permissions → Location. Return here and enable local weather again.

      
      
This briefly opens and closes the glasses microphone to check the connection. No recording is submitted. Even's current SDK does not provide a shortcut to phone permission settings.

    

    
Weather
      
Use the phone location provided by Even to show nearby weather. Rounded coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo only while weather is enabled; your location is not saved.
